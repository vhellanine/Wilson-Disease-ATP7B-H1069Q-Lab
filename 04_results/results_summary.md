# Results Summary

# 1. Wild-Type Reference Sequence

The ATP7B reference transcript used in this analysis was **NM_000053.4**, with reference protein **NP_000044.2**. The WT coding sequence (CDS) was **4,398 nucleotides** long and was translated using SeqKit in Galaxy using **reading frame 1** and the standard genetic code.

The predicted WT protein was **1,465 amino acids** long. The sequence began with the start codon **ATG** and ended with the stop codon **TGA**. The first 10 amino acids were **MPEQERQITA**, and the last 10 amino acids were **LNGRDEEQYI**.

The predicted WT protein was compared with the accepted reference ATP7B protein using MAFFT protein sequence alignment.

# 2. Documented Mutation

The documented ATP7B variant investigated was **NM_000053.4:c.3207C>A**, resulting in the protein change **p.His1069Gln (H1069Q)**.

The mutation changed one nucleotide at CDS position **3207**, changing the codon from **CAC** to **CAA**. This resulted in a single amino acid substitution from **histidine (H) to glutamine (Q) at position 1069**.

The mutant CDS remained **4,398 nucleotides** long and was translated in **reading frame 1**, producing a predicted protein of **1,465 amino acids**. No premature stop codon was observed. Therefore, the mutation did not change the overall protein length or reading frame.

## 3. WT and Documented Mutant Protein Comparison

The predicted WT and documented mutant ATP7B proteins were compared using sequence alignment. The first amino acid difference occurred at **position 1069**, where **H was replaced by Q**.

Only the amino acid at the documented mutation site was directly changed in the predicted sequence. No frameshift or premature termination was observed.

The results therefore indicate that the c.3207C>A substitution produces a **missense mutation** at the protein-sequence level while maintaining the same predicted protein length.

# 4. Artificial Mutation

A student-created **three-nucleotide deletion** was performed at CDS positions **c.25_27**, removing the sequence **ACA**, which corresponds to **Thr9**.

The artificial mutant CDS was predicted to have a length of **4,395 nucleotides**, compared with 4,398 nucleotides in the WT CDS. Because exactly three nucleotides were deleted, the reading frame remained unchanged.

The predicted artificial mutant protein was expected to be **1,464 amino acids** long, representing the deletion of one amino acid, **Thr9**. No premature stop codon was expected.

## 5. Comparison of the Documented and Artificial Mutations

The documented and artificial mutations produced different sequence-level effects because they differed in mutation type and location.

The documented **c.3207C>A** mutation is a single-nucleotide substitution that changes one amino acid (**H1069Q**) without changing the reading frame or protein length. In contrast, the artificial **c.25_27del** mutation removes three nucleotides and deletes one amino acid (**Thr9**) while preserving the reading frame.

These results demonstrate that the effect of a mutation depends on its **type, size, and exact position within the coding sequence**.

# 6. Overall Result

The sequence analysis demonstrated the relationship between nucleotide changes and their predicted protein consequences. The documented ATP7B H1069Q variant produced a single amino acid substitution, whereas the artificial three-nucleotide deletion removed one amino acid without causing a frameshift.

The computational results support conclusions about the nucleotide and predicted protein sequence changes. However, sequence analysis alone does not establish actual protein expression, stability, localization, folding, or ATP7B copper-transport activity.
