# Activity: From Gene Mutation to Disease

# Disease / Phenotype

*Disease:* Wilson Disease  
*Gene:* ATP7B

Wilson disease is an inherited disorder of copper metabolism caused by pathogenic variants in the ATP7B gene. It results in abnormal copper accumulation, particularly in the liver, brain, and eyes.

# Reference Sequence

*Reference Transcript:* NM_000053.4  
*Reference Protein:* NP_000044.2  
*Protein:* Copper-transporting ATPase 2 (ATP7B)  
*Reference Protein Length:* 1,465 amino acids

The normal ATP7B coding sequence (CDS) was obtained from NCBI and translated using Galaxy to generate the predicted wild-type protein sequence.

# Documented Mutation

*Variant:* NM_000053.4:c.3207C>A  
*Protein Change:* p.His1069Gln (H1069Q)  
*Mutation Type:* Single-nucleotide substitution / Missense variant  
*Original Codon:* CAC  
*Mutant Codon:* CAA  
*Nucleotide Position:* 3207  
*ClinVar Variation ID:* 3848

The documented mutation was reproduced by changing the nucleotide at CDS position 3207 from C to A in a copy of the wild-type ATP7B CDS.

# Artificial Mutation
A student-created artificial mutation was also performed to examine the effect of an in-frame deletion.

*Artificial Variant:* NM_000053.4:c.25_27del  
*Mutation Type:* Three-nucleotide deletion  
*Deleted Sequence:* ACA  
*Affected Amino Acid:* Thr9  
*Predicted Protein Change:* p.(Thr9del)

The artificial mutation was created by deleting exactly three nucleotides from the wild-type CDS. Because three nucleotides were removed, the reading frame was predicted to remain unchanged.

# Galaxy Analysis
*Galaxy History:* Olaguir_Wilson_ATP7B_H1069Q_Lab

The following sequence analyses were performed in Galaxy:

1. WT ATP7B CDS translation
2. Comparison of the predicted WT protein with the accepted reference protein
3. Reproduction of the documented c.3207C>A mutation
4. Translation of the documented mutant CDS
5. Comparison of WT and documented mutant proteins
6. Creation of the artificial three-nucleotide deletion
7. Translation and analysis of the artificial mutant

# Expected Molecular Effects

The documented ATP7B c.3207C>A variant changes the codon from CAC to CAA, resulting in a predicted amino acid substitution from histidine (H) to glutamine (Q) at position 1069. The reading frame and protein length remain unchanged.

The artificial c.25_27del mutation removes one complete codon, resulting in the predicted deletion of Thr9 while maintaining the reading frame.
